## S1: bugs that would reach a rider

Ordered by impact. The first eight would each block a beta on their own.

1. **Bike page crashes on every open.** `components/garage/BikePage.tsx:86` calls `useState` (the rename state) after the early returns at `:63-76`. First render registers N hooks, the render after data loads registers N+1, React throws "Rendered more hooks than during the previous render". This is `/garage-bike`, the landing of every bike card tap and of both Garage quiz flows. Uncommitted, never device-run. Fix: move the line above the first `return`. CONFIRMED (x3, also flagged by lint).

2. **Nothing from a ride day ever reaches the server.** `lib/rideDay.ts:386` and `:413` upsert with `onConflict: "local_id"`, but the tables declare `unique (user_id, local_id)` (`supabase/migrations/20260904120000_ride_day.sql:58,122`). Postgres infers a conflict arbiter only from an index on exactly the listed columns, so every `ride_day_upsert`, `moto_insert` and `ride_day_end` job fails with 42P10 and stays queued forever. Consequences: "saved on phone" forever, `setup_versions.ride_day_id` never set, `match_tracks.rider_count` always 0, and `_trial_ride_days` (`20260904150000:52-64`) always 0, so the reverse trial's ride-day leg and the lifetime offer never fire and every trial ends by the 21-day clock. Fix: `onConflict: "user_id,local_id"` at both sites (the idiom `lib/seasonGoals.ts:92` already uses). CONFIRMED (x3, Postgres arbiter rule; the jest suite pins no `onConflict`).

3. **Named setups cannot get a version.** `20260904100000_home_garage_v3.sql:134-150` scopes `version_number` per `(user_id, bike_id, setup_id)`, but production still carries `unique (bike_id, version_number)` from `20260706120000_tune_two_lineage.sql:36`, never dropped. The first version of any named setup on a bike with a baseline gets number 1 again and fails with 23505. All three writers swallow it: `lib/bikeSetups.ts:226-233` (setup exists with zero versions), `lib/autoBaseline.ts:191-195` via the Garage "New setup" reveal, `lib/rideEnd.ts:65` (settle on a named setup, "kept local"). The branch device pass could not surface this. Fix: drop the constraint and add two partial unique indexes, `(bike_id, version_number) where setup_id is null` and `(bike_id, setup_id, version_number) where setup_id is not null`, which keeps today's guarantee byte for byte and stays safe on populated rows. CONFIRMED.

Nothing in 3.0 has been device-verified end to end; the two device passes this week found real defects in every screen they reached, and this audit found eight more that a device pass could not have surfaced because the writers swallow their errors (findings 2, 3, 11, 15) or because they need existing accounts and named setups (4, 6, 7, 14).

Effort to a beta that a handful of riders could use for a weekend without losing data:

- The ten fixes above: about eight to ten focused evenings. Items 1, 2 and 17 are minutes; 3, 4, 7 and 8 are half an evening each; 5, 6, 9 and 10 are an evening each because they touch several files and need tests.
- Two more device passes after those land (Garage with named setups and an existing account; a full ride day offline and online with the sync verified in the branch tables): two to three sessions, plus fixes.
- External work with no code: RevenueCat products, offering and placements, intro-trial removal, the OpenAI key on any new environment, the migration push sequence, the hotfix merge back to main and the air-display reconcile.

Roughly two weeks of evenings from here to a TestFlight beta, if the passes go well and the decisions above are made early enough not to reopen code. A store build is a separate step after the beta week: the lifecycle function, dashboards and native build checklist can trail the beta, but the eight blockers cannot.
